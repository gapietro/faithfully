## What this does not cover

**The scripture text itself is public domain and no rights are claimed over
it.** Faithfully ships the World English Bible (WEB) and the King James Version
(KJV), both public domain — see
[`docs/content/TRANSLATION_LICENSING.md`](docs/content/TRANSLATION_LICENSING.md).
Anyone may use those verses freely, from any source.

What is claimed is the original work around them: which verse belongs to which
day, the challenge written against it, the prompt, and the arrangement of the
365 as a set. That is the editorial work, and it is the part that took the time.

## Why the split

The code is more useful to other people than it is valuable to keep closed —
the civil-day model, the persistence contract, the accessibility work, and the
`make ci` gate are all worth borrowing, and borrowing them costs this project
nothing.

The content is the product. Publishing the repository should not mean handing
someone a finished competing app.

If you want to use the content, open an issue. The answer is not automatically
no — it is just not automatically yes.
