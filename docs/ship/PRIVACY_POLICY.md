**Last updated: July 20, 2026**

Faithfully is a daily Christian action app. We built it so that your spiritual life stays between you and God — not us.

## No account, no sign-up

Faithfully does not ask you to create an account. There is no login, no email collection, and no registration of any kind.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything the app stores — which challenges you've completed, your journal reflections, your streaks and badges, your settings — is saved only on your iPhone. We do not have servers, we do not receive your data, and we cannot see it.

If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple may include the app's data in your encrypted device backup like any other app; that backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and we still cannot see it.

## No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Faithfully contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs of any kind. The app is built entirely with Apple's native frameworks. We do not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, or crash data beyond what Apple provides to all developers through the standard App Store opt-in diagnostics.

## Notifications are optional

If you allow notifications, the app schedules daily reminders locally on your device (for example, your morning challenge and an evening reminder). These are generated on your phone, not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

## Scripture text

Bible text displayed in the app is used under the respective publishers' terms. Those rights are between the app and the publishers and do not involve any of your personal data.

## Children

Faithfully does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, adding optional iCloud sync), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before that version ships.
